# Standard Hong Kong Hotel Check-In, Check-Out & Cancellation FAQ

- **Standard Check-In Time**: **15:00 (3:00 PM)** across most Hong Kong hotels (14:00 at select properties like Hotel ICON).
- **Standard Check-Out Time**: **11:00 AM or 12:00 PM (Noon)**.
- **Late Check-Out Policy**:
  - Complimentary until 13:00–14:00 subject to availability (or guaranteed for Club/Elite members).
  - Until 18:00 (6:00 PM): Typically charged at **50% of the Best Available Rate**.
  - After 18:00: Charged at **100% of one night's room rate**.
- **Luggage Storage**: All listed Hong Kong hotels offer **free luggage storage** at the Concierge desk before check-in and after check-out.
- **Cancellation Terms**:
  - *Flexible Rate*: Free cancellation up to 24–48 hours prior to arrival (18:00 HKT).
  - *Advance Purchase / Non-Refundable*: Full prepayment required; no refund upon cancellation or modification.
