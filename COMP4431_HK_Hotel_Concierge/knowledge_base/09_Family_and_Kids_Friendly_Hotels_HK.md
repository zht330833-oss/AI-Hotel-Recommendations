# Family & Kids-Friendly Hotels Guide (Hong Kong)

## Best Picks for Families Traveling with Children
1. **Hong Kong Disneyland Hotel (Lantau, HK$3,100+)**: Dedicated Kingdom Club themed rooms, Disney character dining, and kids' activities.
2. **Kerry Hotel Hong Kong (Hung Hom, HK$1,950+)**: Spacious rooms (42–52 sqm) easily accommodating extra rollaway beds or baby cribs; safe waterfront promenade outside.
3. **Cordis Hong Kong (Mong Kok, HK$1,550+)**: Offers dedicated Family Two-Bedroom Suites and kids' welcome amenities.
4. **Hotel ICON (Hung Hom, HK$1,650+)**: Family-friendly Club rooms, complimentary mini-bar snacks/drinks, and heated pool.
5. **The Upper House (Admiralty, HK$4,900+)**: Standard rooms start at 68 sqm (largest in HK), providing ample space for families with infants without needing a suite upgrade.

## Standard Family Policies
- Children aged **11 and under** usually stay free when sharing existing bedding.
- Baby cots/cribs are complimentary upon advance request; extra rollaway beds typically cost **HK$400–HK$650 + 13% tax/service per night**.
