# District Guide: Hung Hom & Mong Kok (Kowloon)

## Overview
Offers exceptional value-for-money luxury harbour views (Hung Hom) and authentic street-market energy (Mong Kok).

## Top Recommended Hotels
- **Hotel ICON (HK$1,650+)**: Wholly owned by The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU) as a teaching and research hotel. Famous for its heated rooftop pool, complimentary in-room mini-bar, Above & Beyond Cantonese dining, and sustainable design.
- **Kerry Hotel Hong Kong (HK$1,950+)**: Urban waterfront resort in Hung Hom Bay with larger rooms than TST counterparts, huge outdoor podium gardens, and a 25-meter infinity pool.
- **Cordis Hong Kong (HK$1,550+)**: Directly linked to Langham Place Mall and Mong Kok MTR Station. Ideal for shoppers and foodies exploring Temple Street and Sneaker Street.
