# Business Travel & MICE Hotels Guide (Hong Kong)

## Top Business Hotels by Key Hub
- **Central Financial District**: **Four Seasons Hotel Hong Kong** & **Mandarin Oriental Hong Kong** — walking distance to Exchange Square, IFC, and Central banking headquarters.
- **Admiralty Government & Legal Hub**: **JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong** — 4-line MTR hub (Island, Tsuen Wan, South Island, East Rail lines) plus large pillarless ballroom for conferences.
- **HKCEC Convention Visitors (Wan Chai)**: **The St. Regis Hong Kong** — 4-minute walk to Exhibition Centre MTR and HKCEC; 24-hour butler pressing service for suits.
- **PolyU / Hung Hom Academic Visitors**: **Hotel ICON** — 5-minute walk to The Hong Kong Polytechnic University campus; Silverbox ballroom and executive lounge.
