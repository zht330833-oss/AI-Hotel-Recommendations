# District Guide: Central, Admiralty & Sheung Wan (Hong Kong Island)

## Overview
The financial, diplomatic, and heritage heart of Hong Kong Island, offering unmatched MTR interchange access and Airport Express connectivity.

## Top Recommended Hotels
- **Four Seasons Hotel Hong Kong (HK$5,500+)**: Directly connected to IFC Mall and Hong Kong Station (Airport Express). Ideal for C-suite business executives and gourmet travellers.
- **The Upper House (HK$4,900+)**: Located above Pacific Place in Admiralty. Features residential-style rooms starting at 68 sqm with serene harbour or mountain views.
- **Mandarin Oriental Hong Kong (HK$4,500+)**: Classic luxury in Central with legendary service and indoor heated lap pool.
- **JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong (HK$2,800+)**: Top choice for business conferences (MICE) with direct access to 4 MTR lines at Admiralty Station.
- **ibis Hong Kong Central & Sheung Wan (HK$880+)**: Best value mid-budget option with harbour-view rooms, Ding Ding tram stop outside, and 5-minute walk to Hong Kong Macau Ferry Terminal.
- **Mini Central (HK$620+)**: Ultra-budget compact rooms on Ice House Street for solo travellers prioritizing location over room size.
