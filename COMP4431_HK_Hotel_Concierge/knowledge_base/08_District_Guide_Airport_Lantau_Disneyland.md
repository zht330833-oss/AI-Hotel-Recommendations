# District Guide: Lantau Island, Airport & Disneyland

## Overview
Best for families visiting Hong Kong Disneyland, Ngong Ping 360, or travellers with early-morning/late-night flights.

## Top Recommended Hotels
- **Hong Kong Disneyland Hotel (HK$3,100+)**: Victorian-style flagship resort with Disney character breakfasts, hedge maze, indoor/outdoor pools, and free shuttle to the theme park.
- **Novotel Citygate Hong Kong (HK$1,150+)**: Directly connected to Tung Chung MTR Station and Citygate Outlets; offers a **complimentary 5-minute shuttle bus to Hong Kong International Airport (HKIA)** every 15–20 minutes.
