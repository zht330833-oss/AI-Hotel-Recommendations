# Hong Kong Single-Use Plastic Regulations in Hotels (2026 Guide)

## 1. Product Eco-responsibility Ordinance Compliance
- Under Hong Kong law, hotels and guesthouses are **prohibited from providing free disposable plastic toiletries and in-room plastic-bottled water**.
- **Banned Free Items**: Plastic-handled toothbrushes, plastic-packaged toothpaste, disposable plastic shower caps, plastic razors, plastic combs, and single-use plastic water bottles.

## 2. How Hong Kong Hotels Adapt
- **Eco-Friendly Replacements**: Luxury and mid-range hotels (e.g., Hotel ICON, Rosewood, Kerry Hotel, Novotel Citygate) provide complimentary **bamboo/wooden toothbrushes, paper-packaged dental kits, refillable large pump dispensers** for shampoo/body wash, and **in-room glass carafes or filtered water dispensers**.
- **Paid Amenity Kits**: Some budget hotels charge a nominal fee (typically **HK$10–HK$30**) if guests request extra disposable amenity kits at reception.
- **Traveler Recommendation**: Guests are encouraged to bring their own personal toothbrush and reusable water bottle, though essential non-plastic items are readily available upon request.
