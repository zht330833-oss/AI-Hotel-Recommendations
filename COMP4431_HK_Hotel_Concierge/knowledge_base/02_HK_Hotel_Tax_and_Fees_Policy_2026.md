# Hong Kong Hotel Accommodation Tax (HAT) & Service Charge Policy (2026)

## 1. Reinstatement of the 3% Hotel Accommodation Tax (HAT)
- **Effective Date**: Reinstated on **1 January 2025** under the Hotel Accommodation Tax Ordinance (Cap. 348) and fully active throughout **2026/27**.
- **Tax Rate**: **3%** levied strictly on the net accommodation charge (room rate) payable by guests.
- **Service Charge Exemption from Tax**: The standard **10% hotel service charge** is NOT subject to the 3% HAT.
- **Calculation Formula**:
  - `Service Charge = Base_Room_Rate * 10%`
  - `Hotel Accommodation Tax (HAT) = Base_Room_Rate * 3%`
  - `Total Nightly Price = Base_Room_Rate * 1.13`
  - *Example*: For a HK$1,000 base room rate, the service charge is HK$100 and HAT is HK$30, making the total payable HK$1,130 per night.

## 2. Exemptions from Hotel Accommodation Tax
- **Long-Term Stays**: Guests staying in the same hotel for **28 consecutive nights or more** are exempt from the 3% HAT.
- **Small Guesthouses**: Establishments with fewer than 10 rooms normally available for guest lodging.
- **Low-Rate Accommodation**: Accommodation charges below HK$15 per day.

## 3. Security Deposit Policy
- Most 4-star and 5-star Hong Kong hotels require a refundable incidentals deposit of **HK$500 to HK$1,500 per night** upon check-in via credit card pre-authorization or cash.
