# Best Budget & Boutique Hotels in Hong Kong (Under HK$1,300/night)

1. **Mini Central (Central, HK$620/night)**: Best for solo backpackers or nightlife visitors wanting to walk to Lan Kwai Fong and Central MTR. Very compact rooms (10–13 sqm).
2. **Bishop Lei International House (Mid-Levels, HK$820/night)**: Hidden gem offering harbour views and an outdoor swimming pool at budget prices, plus free shuttle bus to Central.
3. **ibis Hong Kong Central & Sheung Wan (Sheung Wan, HK$880/night)**: Reliable international 3.5-star standard, harbour view rooms available, tram stop right outside.
4. **Novotel Citygate Hong Kong (Tung Chung, HK$1,150/night)**: Best sub-HK$1,200 option near Hong Kong International Airport and AsiaWorld-Expo.
5. **AKI Hong Kong - MGallery (Wan Chai, HK$1,250/night)**: Design-led boutique hotel with unique Japanese Tatami rooms.
