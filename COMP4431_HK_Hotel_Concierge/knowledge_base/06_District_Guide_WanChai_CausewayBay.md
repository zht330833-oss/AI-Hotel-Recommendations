# District Guide: Wan Chai & Causeway Bay (Hong Kong Island)

## Overview
Ideal for exhibition visitors (HKCEC), retail therapy, and vibrant late-night dining.

## Top Recommended Hotels
- **The St. Regis Hong Kong (HK$4,200+)**: Quiet residential luxury in Wan Chai with 24-hour signature Butler Service and two Michelin-starred restaurants. Walking distance to Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre (HKCEC).
- **The Park Lane Hong Kong, a Pullman Hotel (HK$1,600+)**: Overlooks Victoria Park in Causeway Bay; features SKYE rooftop bar, pet-friendly floors, and immediate access to SOGO and Times Square.
- **AKI Hong Kong - MGallery (HK$1,250+)**: Stylish boutique hotel in Wan Chai featuring unique Japanese Tatami rooms and eco-conscious design, great for solo travellers and couples.
