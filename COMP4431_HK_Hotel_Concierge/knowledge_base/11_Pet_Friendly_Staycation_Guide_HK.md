# Pet-Friendly Hotels & Staycation Guide (Hong Kong)

## Verified Pet-Friendly Hotels in Hong Kong
1. **Rosewood Hong Kong (Tsim Sha Tsui, HK$5,800+)**: Welcomes both dogs and cats; provides plush pet beds, food/water bowls, and treats; located right next to the pet-friendly Victoria Dockside waterfront promenade.
2. **W Hong Kong (West Kowloon, HK$2,900+)**: Offers the "P.A.W. (Pets Are Welcome)" package for dogs/cats up to 15 kg; near West Kowloon Art Park (pet-friendly lawn).
3. **Four Seasons Hotel Hong Kong (Central, HK$5,500+)**: Welcomes small dogs under 7 kg with bespoke pet amenities.
4. **The Park Lane Hong Kong (Causeway Bay, HK$1,600+)**: Mid-range pet-friendly rooms opposite Victoria Park; cleaning fee of around HK$500 per stay applies.

## Important Pet Stay Rules
- Advance notice (at least 48 hours before check-in) is mandatory.
- Pets are generally not permitted in swimming pool areas or indoor restaurants (except designated outdoor pet-friendly terraces).
