# Airport Express & MTR Connectivity Guide for Hotel Guests

## Fastest Airport Transfers from Hong Kong International Airport (HKIA)
- **Airport Express Train (24 minutes to Hong Kong Station)**:
  - Best hotels at **Kowloon Station**: **W Hong Kong** (direct indoor elevator access).
  - Best hotels at **Hong Kong Station (Central)**: **Four Seasons Hotel Hong Kong** (direct indoor walkway), **Mandarin Oriental** (5-min walk).
- **Free Hotel Airport Shuttle (5 minutes)**:
  - **Novotel Citygate Hong Kong** provides complimentary shuttle buses directly between HKIA Terminal 1 and the hotel.
- **Taxi / Limousine Estimates from HKIA**:
  - To Tsim Sha Tsui / Hung Hom: ~30–35 mins, ~HK$280–HK$330 (urban red taxi + bridge tolls).
  - To Central / Wan Chai / Causeway Bay: ~35–45 mins, ~HK$350–HK$400.
