# District Guide: Tsim Sha Tsui & West Kowloon (Kowloon)

## Overview
Tsim Sha Tsui (TST) and West Kowloon form Hong Kong's premier harbourfront cultural, luxury shopping, and museum district.

## Top Recommended Hotels
- **Rosewood Hong Kong (HK$5,800+)**: Best for ultra-luxury travellers, art lovers, and pet owners. Direct indoor access to K11 MUSEA and Avenue of Stars.
- **Regent Hong Kong (HK$4,600+)**: Best for unobstructed panoramic Victoria Harbour views and Michelin-starred Cantonese dining.
- **The Peninsula Hong Kong (HK$5,200+)**: Best for heritage luxury, iconic Lobby Afternoon Tea, and Rolls-Royce airport transfers.
- **The Langham Hong Kong (HK$2,400+)**: Best for luxury shoppers wanting immediate access to Canton Road flagship stores at a lower rate than harbourfront icons.
- **W Hong Kong (HK$2,900+)**: Best for stylish leisure travellers and seamless Airport Express connectivity at Kowloon Station (24 minutes to HKIA).

## Transport & Attractions
- **MTR Stations**: Tsim Sha Tsui (Tsuen Wan Line), East Tsim Sha Tsui (Tuen Ma Line), Kowloon Station (Airport Express & Tung Chung Line).
- **Key Landmarks**: Hong Kong Palace Museum, M+ Museum, Victoria Dockside, Star Ferry Pier, Harbour City.
